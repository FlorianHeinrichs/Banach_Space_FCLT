README – DWD 10‑minute Air Temperature Data (Aachen, Bochum, Göttingen)

These files contain 10‑minute station measurements of air temperature and related variables from the Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD) Climate Data Center for stations in Aachen, Bochum and Göttingen. 
The dataset provides time series from different time horizons for the three locations, including air temperature at 2 m, temperature at 5 cm, relative humidity, dew point temperature, and air pressure at station height. 

Features:
- date,air pressure in hPa,temperature at 2 m in C,temperature at 5 cm in C,relative humidity,dew point temperature in C

This dataset is a processed subset/derivative of: "10‑minütige Stationsmessungen der Lufttemperatur für Deutschland, Version v24.03, Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), Climate Data Center (CDC).".

Please cite the data as:  
"10‑minütige Stationsmessungen der Lufttemperatur für Deutschland, Version v24.03, Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), Climate Data Center (CDC)."
